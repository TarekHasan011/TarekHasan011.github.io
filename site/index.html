June – September 2024 · School of Computing, National University of Singapore

Summer Research Intern

Advisor: Assoc. Prof. Terence Sim · Mentors: Dr. Sanjay Saha and Dr. Sanka Rasnayaka

−25.98%average Equal Error Rate
  • One-shot biometric authentication. I proposed GenAI Augmented Enrollment (GAE). A Stable Diffusion face-animation model generates pose- and expression-varied samples from a single photo, and their embeddings are averaged into a robust biometric signature.
  • Benchmarked across six face recognition models (ArcFace, FaceNet, FaceNet512, GhostFaceNet, SFace, VGG-Face) on CASIA-WebFace, LFW and VoxCeleb. The largest gains were for non-frontal enrollment.
  • Deepfake synthesis vs. detection. I generated talking-head deepfakes with GAN, diffusion and NeRF methods and benchmarked state-of-the-art detectors. They generalise poorly to unseen diffusion-based generators.
  • I ran a human perception study on the same videos for a direct people-vs-detector comparison. The result is the preprint Deepfake Synthesis vs. Detection: An Uneven Contest (2026).

Where do these numbers come from?
  • Citations, h-index and the publication list are read from Google Scholar each time the page loads. Refreshes within 10 minutes reuse the last result so Scholar doesn't block the site.
  • Scopus quartile is read live from Elsevier's Scopus API (CiteScore percentile) when an API key is configured. Otherwise it shows the quartile recorded in the CV.
  • JCR Impact Factor is Clarivate's proprietary figure and has no public API. It is shown as recorded in the CV.
  • 2-yr citedness is fetched live from OpenAlex. It uses the same formula as an impact factor, but on OpenAlex data, so values differ slightly from JCR.
